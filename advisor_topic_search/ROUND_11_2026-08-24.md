# 2. NEW TOP EXECUTABLE SCIENCE — Positional Imprinting of Parametric Knowledge

## Working title

**Does Factual Knowledge Remember Where It Was First Learned?**

Scientific framing:

**Positional Imprinting and Path Dependence in Parametric Knowledge Acquisition**

## 2.1 Seed

NAACL 2025 Main Long Paper / Oral:

**Where is the answer? An empirical study of positional bias for parametric knowledge extraction in language model** — Kuniaki Saito, Chen-Yu Lee, Kihyuk Sohn, Yoshitaka Ushiku.

Official repository:

`https://github.com/omron-sinicx/WhereIsTheAnswer`

Verified upstream commit:

`910fcddec93f7400b58257d70abf1dab31f1e179`

The seed studies new factual knowledge acquired by continued training. It moves the same answer-bearing sentence to different positions in training documents and reports a large positional extraction bias: facts learned near the beginning are far more extractable by later closed-book QA than the same facts placed later in the document.

The paper further shows:

- the phenomenon occurs on Wiki2023+ and synthetic data;
- it survives longer training under vanilla AR;
- larger models reduce but do not remove it;
- D-AR and other regularizers mitigate it;
- sentence shuffling, which exposes content under varying preceding contexts, also improves extraction.

The seed already owns:

- `document position affects final parametric knowledge extraction`;
- `AR previous-token dependence is a major explanation`;
- `D-AR / shuffling / attention dropout can mitigate the position bias`.

Do **not** repackage those claims as our novelty.

## 2.2 Exact one-step new question

The seed varies the **position distribution** under which a fact is learned.

The missing learning-history question is stricter:

> **If every fact is ultimately exposed exactly the same number of times at an early and a late document position, and the final training tail is also identical, does the position at which the fact was first/early encoded leave a persistent effect on its final extractability?**

This is not generic curriculum learning and not `position matters`.

It tests whether the **history of acquisition context** leaves a durable imprint after aggregate exposure statistics have been equalized.

A matched schedule family can be:

```text
positions shown for the same target fact

EARLY-FIRST:  1, 1, 5, 5,  1, 5, 1, 5
LATE-FIRST:   5, 5, 1, 1,  1, 5, 1, 5
INTERLEAVED:  1, 5, 1, 5,  1, 5, 1, 5
```

All three conditions have:

```text
same target facts
same document text variants
same number of P1 exposures
same number of P5 exposures
same total tokens
same QA examples
same optimizer budget
same final four exposure positions
```

Only the early acquisition history differs.

The identical final tail is important: it prevents the result from being reduced to `the last exposure wins`.

## 2.3 Why a positive result is exciting

If final closed-book QA remains different after the exact positional multiset and recent training tail are matched, then factual memory is not determined only by **what** a model saw or even by **where** it saw it in aggregate.

The trajectory of encoding matters:

> **the same evidence can produce different endpoint memories because the acquisition context arrived in a different order.**

This would turn the seed's static positional bias into a developmental/path-dependence phenomenon about parametric memory formation.

A clean null is equally useful for killing this formulation: if the matched washout tail erases the difference, then the seed phenomenon is exposure-distribution dependent rather than historically imprinted.

Use the same facts and same P1/P5 document variants under matched exposure multisets.

Preferred schedules:


The `+ 1,5,1,5` tail is identical in every condition.

## 6.2 Primary endpoint

Final closed-book QA on the same held-out QA interface used by the seed.

```text
Delta_history = Acc(early-first) - Acc(late-first)
```

Secondary:

- early-first vs interleaved;
- late-first vs interleaved;

## 6.3 Frozen scientific gate

Before running the new history experiment, freeze a practical effect-size gate.

Recommended starting policy for the preregistration, subject to power calculation using only seed variance / pilot-independent information:

```text
paper-interest positive:
  absolute paired history effect >= 5 pp
  AND uncertainty excludes 0
  AND same qualitative direction in >= 2 preregistered training seeds

strong null / kill target:
  equivalence inside a +/-3 pp practical region after the identical washout tail
```

Do not change the region after seeing results.

If the first clean run lands between the positive and equivalence gates, mark `INCONCLUSIVE`; do not model-shop.

## 6.4 What kills the mother formulation

Kill **Positional Imprinting** if, after seed reproduction and matched scheduling:

- endpoint performance is equivalent across early-first / late-first under the preregistered practical bound; or
- any apparent difference disappears once the recent tail and exposure counts are exactly matched; or
- the result is not stable across the frozen seeds;
- schedule control cannot be guaranteed without changing the training object beyond recognition.

Do not rescue with another model, another answer position, another subset, or a new metric.

# 7. Research Branch Map — if G0 stands

## Branch A — behavioral law / washout

- How long does early positional history survive?
- Does the effect decay smoothly under identical later exposure?
- Is early-good -> late-bad symmetric with late-bad -> early-good?
- Does the effect depend on the size of the original seed positional gap?

This branch requires no hidden-state story.

## Branch B — storage vs access

For endpoint-matched facts that fail ordinary QA, reinstate the context that accompanied their early acquisition.

Question:

> is weak extraction true storage failure, or is the fact accessible under an encoding-congruent cue?

This must be a frozen cue family, not prompt fishing.

## Branch C — development / critical window

Move the history split to different portions of training while preserving the final exposure multiset.

Question:

> is there an early window in which document context has disproportionate influence on the eventual memory trace?

A `critical window` null does not kill Branch A/B if persistent history dependence still exists.

## Branch D — representation / mechanism

Only after behavioral history dependence is established:

- compare endpoint models trained on the same exact multiset but different histories;
- test whether ordinary QA and context-reinstated access use different internal routes;
- use bounded causal tests rather than broad probe/layer fishing.

## Branch E — intervention

Test whether training procedures that reduce seed positional dependence also erase history dependence:

- D-AR;
- explicit position rotation;
- controlled sentence shuffling;
- balanced replay.

This distinguishes methods that improve endpoint accuracy from methods that truly remove path dependence.

## Branch F — external generalization

Only after the controlled object stands:

- Wiki2023+ vs synthetic language;
- Llama vs Mistral/Zephyr family differences already exposed by the seed;
- domain-adaptive / continual pretraining.

# 8. Collision audit

## 8.1 Direct seed collision

The NAACL 2025 seed owns static document-position bias and mitigation. Our novelty cannot be `position matters` or `shuffle helps`.

## 8.2 Generic training-order collision

ACL 2024 Main **Instruction-tuned Language Models are Better Knowledge Learners** shows that learning QA before versus after documents changes factual acquisition. Therefore `training order affects factual learning` is already occupied.

Our surviving boundary is narrower and matched:

> **same exact fact-position exposure multiset + identical recent tail + different early positional history.**

## 8.3 Training-order representation collision

**Fresh in Memory: Training-order Recency Is Linearly Encoded in Language Model Activations** shows that models retain information about when data was learned in activation space, and this signal can persist after later training/mixing.

This is an important near neighbor and removes any claim that `LLMs encode training history` by itself is new.

It does **not** directly test whether the *document position of factual encoding* leaves a persistent effect on endpoint closed-book accessibility after the final position-exposure multiset and recent tail are matched.

It also means Branch D must not become `probe when the fact was learned`; that space is already occupied.

## 8.4 Inference serial-position work

Lost-in-the-middle / serial-position / primacy effects at **inference time** are neighboring terminology, not the same object. Avoid selling this as generic `primacy effect`.

Preferred term: **positional imprinting of parametric knowledge acquisition**.

## Collision verdict

No exact 2025–2026 work was located that performs the matched endpoint experiment above.

`SURVIVES EXACT AUDIT, WITH NARROW NOVELTY BOUNDARY`.
